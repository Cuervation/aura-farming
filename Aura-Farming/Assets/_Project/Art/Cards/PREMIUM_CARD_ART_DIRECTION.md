# Aura Farming — Premium Signal Card Art Direction

## North star
**Mystical nocturnal farming meets signal magic.** Every card is a vertical collectible illustration with deep indigo negative space, emerald/teal bioluminescence and restrained antique-gold botanical filigree.

## Card system
- **Frame:** 4% safe border; thin gold-vine ornament; never obscure the central signal.
- **Hierarchy:** Unity overlays the signal value at top and title/effect at bottom; art must preserve dark, quiet negative space in both zones.
- **Progression:** Signal 1 starts as a sprout. Each value increases scale, luminosity, complexity and cosmic reach, ending in a full aurora harvest at Signal 9.
- **Accessibility:** the subject silhouette and glow must remain distinguishable at small mobile sizes; do not rely solely on hue.
- **Consistency:** portrait 2:3, centered subject, no text/logos/numbers baked into imagery.

## Signal progression
1. First Sprout — luminous seedling
2. Root Whisper — radiant roots map
3. Dew Chorus — glowing droplets and plant-song
4. Verdant Circuit — intertwined luminous vines
5. Moonlit Bloom — large flower under moon
6. Harvest Pulse — ripe luminous field

8. Aurora Orchard — orchard under aurora
9. Crown of Seasons — cosmic harvest mandala

## Card back
Symmetrical seed-signal sigil, midnight indigo, teal energy rings and gold leaves; readable when small and without any asymmetric directional clues.
